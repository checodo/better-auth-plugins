## Is this part of better-auth?

No. These are community plugins, made to look and feel like
better-auth's own, but not made or reviewed by the better-auth team.
Report problems in this project's repository, not to better-auth.

## Which better-auth versions are supported?

`better-auth` and `@better-auth/core` 1.7.20 or a later 1.x release. The
packages declare this range as peer dependencies, and it is the version
the tests run against.

## Can I use them with CommonJS?

The packages are ES modules only, like most of the current better-auth
ecosystem. From CommonJS, load them with a dynamic `import()`.

## Why can't phone number, wallet, or Steam users sign up with the waitlist on?

They have generated emails, which are never on the waitlist. Put their
domain in the waitlist's `allow`, or let their requests through with
`bypass`, see [Sign-up](/docs/waitlist#sign-up).

## Do invited organization members skip the waitlist?

No. An organization invitation doesn't approve an email for the
waitlist. Approve the invitee too (`createInvite` on the waitlist), or let
the invitation's sign-up through with `bypass`.

## Why isn't `auth.api.addMember` in the audit log?

`addMember` is server-only: it has no URL, so no plugin hook sees it.
Record the event yourself with
[`recordAuditEvent`](/docs/audit-log#your-own-events) where you call it.

## Where do I report a security problem?

Privately, not in a public issue. See the repository's
[security policy](https://github.com/checodo/better-auth-plugins/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
